PART TWO: POLICIES

Family Leave

Birth parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid leave after a child is born, and partners receive 14 weeks. Adoptive parents receive the same 26 weeks from the placement date. Team members are expected to record decisions in the shared tracker and to raise blockers early. New joiners are paired with a buddy for their first month, and managers review workloads every quarter so that nobody carries an unreasonable amount of work for long. 

Sabbatical

After seven full years of service an employee may take a sabbatical of 9 weeks at 60 percent pay, once in any five-year period. Team members are expected to record decisions in the shared tracker and to raise blockers early. New joiners are paired with a buddy for their first month, and managers review workloads every quarter so that nobody carries an unreasonable amount of work for long.
